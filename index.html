WEEK 01 — LEARNING NOTES

Introducing Digital Practices

What we discussed

This week introduced different ways of understanding the relationship between technology and society.

Key concepts

Technological determinism · Social constructionism

My reflection

Gillespie (2013) shows that algorithms are not neutral tools, but shape public knowledge and culture under the guise of "objectivity." This reminds us to critically question the hidden power and biases behind digital platforms.

Questions to explore

Can technology ever be truly neutral? Who has the power to shape the technologies we use?

Readings & References

Gillespie, T. (2013). The Relevance of Algorithms . In: Gillespie, T., Boczkowski, P. J., and Foot, K. A. eds. Media Technologies: Essays on Communication, Materiality, and Society. Cambridge, MA: MIT Press, pp. 167–193.
